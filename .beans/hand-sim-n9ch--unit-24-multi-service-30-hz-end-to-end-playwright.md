## What to build

Implement a comprehensive multi-service automated end-to-end integration test suite using Playwright and Cucumber-Gherkin. Orchestrate the startup, health checking, and teardown of the mock ROS2 joint publisher, EdgeNode, Gateway, and TeleopClient Vite development server. Verify the full vertical tracer bullet: synthetic 30 Hz joint states generated by the mock publisher flow through EdgeNode mapping, traverse DataFabric over `robot/{id}/telemetry`, multiplex through Gateway to ActiveSession, and hydrate TeleopClient DOM text nodes at ~30 Hz. Assert that latency remains below 50ms, the state transitions to `CONNECTED / IDLE`, all 6 canonical joints are painted accurately, and "Verify connection" controls are removed from the DOM during streaming.

- [ ] Test asserts latency remains under 50ms without frame accumulation or browser freezing.
- [ ] Test asserts all 6 canonical UR5e joint readouts update accurately in the DOM.
- [ ] Test asserts "Verify connection" controls are removed from the DOM once the active telemetry stream is running.
- [ ] Clean process shutdown and resource reclamation on test completion or failure.
