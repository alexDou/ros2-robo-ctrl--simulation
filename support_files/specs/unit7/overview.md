### Multi-Color Gear Sorting & Defect QC Inspection

* **Goal**: Extend pick-and-place with simulated quality-and-color classification plus four-destination routing. A click on the WorkcellTable still places an initially grey Gearwheel for instant feedback; the Gateway enriches the spawn with an assigned color (`WHITE`, `GREEN`, `BLUE`) and a defective flag (roughly 20% defective) before publish. The authoritative `WorkcellState` persists the classification from table through grasp to drop and routes each gear: sound gears to the SpindleTower matching their color, defective gears of any color to the ScrapBin (defective dominates color). TeleopClient recolors each gear on the authoritative echo (grey until echo, then classified color matched by gear id, with a visible crack notch when defective), shows per-tower fill counters (`n/10`), and shows the ScrapBin as an OS-trash-style empty-versus-non-empty indicator with no numeric count. Each tower holds at most 10 gears with oldest-bottom FIFO eviction on the 11th arrival; the bin piles unbounded. `ClearWorkspace` wipes all three towers plus the bin, resets every counter and the bin icon, and lifts placement lockout. Nothing about frequencies, throttling, render rate, lifecycle states, or transport channels changes.
* **Architecture**: Contract-first parallel development. Unit 7.0 locks down color/defective domain contracts, per-tower coordinates, and capacity constants plus regenerated cross-language types. Units 7.1a (Workcell service extension: persist classification), 7.1b (4-destination routing), 7.2 (Gateway `QcClassifier` plugin), and 7.3a-7.3e (TeleopClient: tower fixtures, recolor-on-echo + counters, ScrapBin fixture + icon, defect notch, per-tower FIFO + Clear all-4) execute against mocked interface seams. Unit 7.4 connects everything in a seeded hermetic end-to-end suite.
